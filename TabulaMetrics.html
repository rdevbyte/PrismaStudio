PRIVACY, BUILT IN

Your file stays yours.

Files are parsed and analyzed in browser memory. This app does not upload or persist their contents; only your theme preference is saved in localStorage.

The local sensitive-data heuristic can ask you to confirm, but it is not a PHI detector or security boundary. If hosted, the web host may log ordinary page-request metadata.
